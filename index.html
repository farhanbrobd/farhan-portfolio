Who I Am

I am Farhan Mostafa, a 10th-grade Science student at Ikkshu Gabeshawana High School, Ishwardi, Pabna. Driven by a passion for medical science, technology, and literature, my core ambition is to become a physician dedicated to serving underprivileged and marginalized communities.
